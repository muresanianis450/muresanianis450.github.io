SD Card Backup

Copy everything from a memory card or USB drive into a single ZIP file, straight from your Android phone.

What it does

Connect a card or drive, tap once, and the app reads every file and folder on it and writes them into one ZIP archive in your Downloads folder. You can then keep it, move it, or send it wherever you like.

Privacy

The app requests no permissions at all and has no internet access. It cannot upload, transmit, or report anything — not analytics, not crash reports, nothing. Everything happens on your device.

Read the full privacy policy

Notes

  • Android asks you to select the card once. After that the app finds it automatically.
  • The folder structure of the card is preserved inside the archive.
  • If a backup is too large for your phone, the app offers to write it elsewhere — the card itself, another drive, or cloud storage.
  • Archives are not encrypted. Anyone who receives the file can open it.
